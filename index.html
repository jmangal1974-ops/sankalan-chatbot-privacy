Siddhi Enterprises operates the Mobile Sankalan WhatsApp Lead Chatbot to provide information about the Mobile Sankalan application and assist potential customers. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

When you interact with our WhatsApp chatbot, we may collect the following information:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
